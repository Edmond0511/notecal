How it works

Three lines. One day of food.

1

Type what you ate

Plain language, just like a notes app. Try - chicken breast, 150g or - 2 eggs.

2

AI does the math

Each line is resolved against trusted nutrition databases — with confidence scores and reasoning you can review.

3

Hit your goals

Personalized calorie and macro targets, weight tracking, and cross-device sync when you sign in.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

🧠

AI nutrition resolution

Powered by Google Gemini. Handles brands, prep methods, and portion sizes — even when you forget the grams.

🎯

Personalized goals

A short wizard sets your daily calorie and macro targets using Mifflin–St Jeor and your activity level.

⚖️

Weight tracking

Log weight with optional notes and photos. Trend chart over 30, 60, or 90 days.

☁️

Cross-device sync

Sign in with Google or Apple to keep your entries, goals, and weight history in sync.

📡

Works offline

Log anytime. Entries queue locally and resolve automatically when you're back online.

⭐

Saved entries

Save your go-to meals once, reuse them instantly — no extra API calls, no waiting.

Built for people who hate tracking food.

Local-first. No ads. No selling your data. Just a faster way to know what you're eating.
