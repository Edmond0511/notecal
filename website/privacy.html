Privacy

Effective May 1, 2026

NoteCal ("we", "our", or "the app") is built around a simple idea: tracking food should be fast, and your data should stay yours. This policy explains what we collect, how we store it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

1. Information we collect

We only collect what's needed to make the app work:

  • Account information. If you choose to sign in, we receive your email address and a unique account identifier from Google or Apple via OAuth. We do not receive your password.
  • Food entries. The text you enter (e.g. "chicken breast, 150g") and the nutrition information our system resolves for it.
  • Personal health metrics. If you choose to set up personalized goals, the wizard collects your sex, age, height, weight, activity level, and goal type to compute your daily calorie and macro targets. Body fat percentage is optional.
  • Weight tracking entries. Weight values, optional notes, and the date of each entry.

2. How your data is stored

NoteCal is local-first. Your entries, goals, and history are saved directly on your device using encrypted local storage. The app works fully offline.

If you sign in, your data is synced to our hosted database (Supabase, a managed Postgres provider) so it stays consistent across your devices. Sync is bidirectional and uses row-level security — only you can read or write your own records.

3. AI processing

To resolve nutrition values, the text of your food entries is sent to Google Gemini through our serverless edge function. Frequently requested entries are cached for up to 7 days on our server to reduce repeat AI calls. The cache stores food text and resolved nutrition only — it does not associate cached results with a specific user.

4. Authentication

We use Supabase Auth with Google Sign-In and Apple Sign-In. You can use the app without an account; signing in is required only for cross-device sync.

5. Photos

Photos you attach to weight-tracking entries are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers and they do not sync across devices.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers, brokers, or analytics platforms. The only third parties that process your data are the infrastructure providers required to operate the service:

  • Supabase — hosted database, authentication, and edge functions.
  • Google Gemini — AI nutrition resolution.
  • Apple / Google — only if you sign in with their identity providers.

7. Your rights

You can:

  • Access and edit any entry directly in the app.
  • Delete your account from the in-app settings. This removes all of your records from our servers. Data stored locally on your device is also cleared.
  • Use the app without signing in, in which case no data ever leaves your device.

8. Children's privacy

NoteCal is intended for users 18 and older. The goals wizard requires confirmation that you are at least 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

9. Security

Data in transit is encrypted using TLS. Local storage on your device is protected by the operating system's app sandbox. While no system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, we follow industry-standard practices to protect your information.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above and notify you in the app before the changes take effect.
